先に「10_【新セッション用】依頼文」を読んでください。規格（サイズ、背景、縮尺など）と、作業の流れは、そちらに書いてあります。


────────────────────────
■ 0. プロンプトの組み立て方
────────────────────────
画像を1枚作るときの完成プロンプト ＝ [S 共通スタイル] ＋ [C キャラの記述] ＋ [M 動きの定義] ＋ [R レイアウト指示]
・[C] と [M] は、このドキュメントの該当部分をそのまま貼る。{N}（コマ数）、{CELL}（256 または 384）、{W}（画像の幅 = コマ数 × セル）、{動き名}、{名前} は、実際の値に置き換える。
・毎回、そのキャラの設定画（Driveの「{名前}_設定画.png」）を、参照画像として添付する。敵は「敵兵_8種設定シート.png」を添付する。
・プロンプトは英語のまま使う（画像生成の精度が上がるため）。ファイル名と、報告は日本語。
・最後に「■ 6. 組み立て例」に、完成形を1つ載せています。

■ 2. [R] レイアウト指示（全プロンプトの末尾）
────────────────────────
Using the attached reference image, keep EXACTLY the same character design, proportions, colors and line style in every frame.
Layout: ONE horizontal sprite strip with exactly {N} frames. Each frame is an equal square cell of {CELL}x{CELL} px, so the whole image is exactly {W}x{CELL} px. No gaps and no borders between cells. In every cell: one full-body character, centered horizontally, the soles of the feet (or the horse's hooves) on the same ground line at 90% of the cell height, and the standing height about 80% of the cell height (the same scale in every cell and the same scale as the standing pose in the reference). A crouching, lunging, jumping or falling pose must NOT make the character smaller: the head, torso and legs keep the same size in every frame. Special effects (flames, lightning, slash trails) must not shrink the character; keep them inside the cell. Keep at least 16 px of empty margin between any part of the drawing and the cell edges (top, left, right). Nothing may cross into a neighboring cell, and no part of a neighboring pose may be visible. Every frame differs only slightly from its neighbours so the motion looks smooth and continuous at about 12 frames per second.
Background: exactly flat pure magenta RGB(255,0,255) / #FF00FF everywhere, with no gradient, no purple or gray tint, no vignette, no noise, no ground, no cast shadow.
Do NOT draw any text, letters, numbers, labels, captions, borders, frame lines, grid lines or cell outlines. No empty cells. ONE character and ONE motion only. The character faces RIGHT in strict side view in every frame.
（数値の目安: セル256のとき 立ち姿の高さ約205px、足元 y=230。セル384のとき 立ち姿の高さ約307px、足元 y=346。）

■ 4-1. 歩き（構え移動）8コマ — 主人公・敵兵・ボス共通
戦場なので、腕を振る普通の歩きではなく、武器を構えたまま、低い姿勢で前に進みます。足は左右交互に出ます。
[M-歩き] Task: draw an 8-frame "combat advance" walk cycle: the character moves to the RIGHT while staying in a low, ready combat stance, the weapon held ready the whole time (do not swing the arms like an ordinary walk). Knees bent, hips low, the upper body, head and weapon almost still. The legs must clearly ALTERNATE in this order:
1. Right foot lands in front (contact pose: wide low stance, right leg forward, left leg behind)
2. Weight shifts onto the right foot (body slightly lowered)
3. The left foot passes the right leg (passing pose, legs close together, body slightly higher)
4. The left foot swings forward
5. Left foot lands in front (contact pose: the mirror of frame 1, LEFT leg forward, right leg behind)
6. Weight shifts onto the left foot (body slightly lowered)
7. The right foot passes the left leg (passing pose)
8. The right foot swings forward (leads back into frame 1 so the cycle loops)
In frames 1 and 5 the front and back legs must be swapped. {構えの武器の記述}
構えの武器の記述 {構えの武器の記述} に入れる文:
・幸村: The cross spear is held diagonally forward at a ready angle with both hands.
・景勝: The katana is held ready in front with the point forward-up.
・三成: The matchlock gun is held ready in the right hand pointing forward-up, the war fan in the left hand.
・信長: The matchlock arquebus is held ready across the body with both hands, the katana at the waist.
・信玄: The war fan is held ready in the right hand in front, the left hand near the sword hilt.
・武蔵: The long sword in the right hand points forward-up, the short sword in the left hand is held low.
・足軽槍: The spear is held level forward with both hands. ・足軽刀: The katana is held low in front with both hands. ・弓足軽: The bow is held in the left hand with an arrow nocked. ・鉄砲足軽: The matchlock gun is held diagonally at the chest. ・忍者: Crouched low, the short sword held reversed behind the back.
・ボス: 各ボスの武器を、構えたまま（直政=大太刀、忠勝=槍、家康=金の太刀、義光=鉄棒、秀秋=金の太刀、秀忠=刀、長政=大太刀、正則=薙刀）。

■ 7. うまくいかないときの足し文（作り直しのとき、末尾に足す）
────────────────────────
・文字が入った: ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT, LETTERS, NUMBERS OR LABELS ANYWHERE IN THE IMAGE. Remove every label.
・背景に色むらが出た: The background must be exactly RGB(255,0,255) in every pixel outside the character. No tint, no gradient.
・隣のコマの欠片が見える: Leave at least 24 px of empty magenta on the left and right of every figure. Do not let any weapon, cloth or effect reach the cell edge.
・キャラの大きさがコマごとに違う: The character's head is the same size (about 30 px tall from chin to the top of the helmet body, in a 256 px cell) in every frame. Do not shrink the character for crouching poses or effects.
・足が入れ替わらない（歩き）: The leg that is in front in frame 1 is behind in frame 5, and vice versa. Draw the two contact poses (frames 1 and 5) as mirror images of each other.
・顔や服が設定画と違う: Copy the face, helmet, armor pattern, banner and colors of the attached reference sheet exactly; do not redesign anything.
・待機が全く同じ絵: Make each of the 4 frames slightly different (chest rising and falling, cloth and hair swaying by a few pixels).
